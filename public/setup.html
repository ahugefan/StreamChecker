Welcome to Twitch Live Checker

This app tracks a list of Twitch streamers and shows you who's live, with titles and viewer counts. It can also shout out streamers automatically in your own chat when they go live, if you want that.

This short setup takes about two minutes.

Connect your Twitch app

This app needs its own Twitch application to read stream data. Create one for free at dev.twitch.tv/console, then paste its keys below.

When creating it: Redirect URL http://localhost:3000, Category "Application Integration", Client Type "Confidential". Name it anything unique (Twitch won't allow the word "twitch" in the name).

Log in with Twitch

Click below to open Twitch in your browser and approve access. Shoutouts will be sent from your own account. (You can switch to a separate bot account later in Settings.)

Shoutout message

This message is sent in your chat when a tracked streamer goes live. {username} is replaced automatically.

If you use StreamElements, Nightbot, or a similar bot for your own !so command, keep this message matching that command's format.

Add streamers to track (optional)

Paste usernames or twitch.tv links, one per line. You can always add more later from Settings.
